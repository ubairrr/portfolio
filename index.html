About Me
“Most software processes data. I build systems that understand it — and act on it.”

Ubair Mustafa

AI & Software Engineer · Firmware to AI Systems · Delhi, India

I build AI systems across the entire stack, and I'm interested in every layer of them — a production multilingual voice agent serving phone calls and web sessions through one code path, a local-first macOS meeting assistant with AES-256 encrypted storage, and register-level embedded C++ on dual-core microcontrollers. I implement things from first principles: my TOTP engine is built from raw HMAC primitives and passes every official RFC 6238 test vector.

I care as much about the guardrails as the features — schema-constrained tool calling, anti-hallucination grounding pipelines, context-window management, and eval harnesses backed by 700+ automated tests. Currently pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science & AI at Jamia Hamdard University.
